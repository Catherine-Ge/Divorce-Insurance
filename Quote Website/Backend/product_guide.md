# Divorce Insurance — Product Guide

## Product overview
Divorce Insurance is a demo insurance product that provides a personalized quote based on age group, province, and years together. Users can receive a quote on the website or through the chat assistant, review their quote details, and request a copy by email.

### How do I get a quote?
Select your age group, province, and how many years you and your partner have been together. Use the quote form or select “Get a quote” in the chat.

### What information do you need?
We need your age group, province or territory, and years together. An email address is needed only if you want your quote emailed. Use the designated quote fields rather than entering personal information in an AI FAQ message.

### How is my premium calculated?
The pricing model uses your selected information to calculate a risk score and determine your premium. The quoted premium represents a single upfront payment, rather than monthly installments.

### What does the risk score mean?
It is an output from the demo's statistical model, trained on simulated data. It is not a reliable prediction of what will happen to your relationship.

### What payout does the quote show?
The demo displays $10,000 when the model's risk score is greater than 50%, and $5,000 when the score is 50% or below.

### Can you explain my quote?
The chat displays the premium and payout calculated by the pricing backend and explains the formula. Do not claim that a particular input caused a specific increase or decrease unless the backend provides that analysis.

### Can I receive my quote by email?
After reviewing your quote, provide an email address and request a copy. If email submission fails, your quote remains available on the screen. Check your spam folder if the email does not appear in your inbox.

### Can I purchase a policy?
Purchasing and policy issuance are not available in this demo.

### When would coverage begin?
This is a demo project, so coverage dates and waiting periods are not available.

### What exclusions apply?
This is a demo project and does not include a full set of policy terms or exclusions.

### How would I make a claim?
This is a demo project, so claims cannot be submitted.

### How do I start again?
Select “Start over” to clear the current chat and quote inputs. This does not recall emails already sent or delete records held by external service providers.
